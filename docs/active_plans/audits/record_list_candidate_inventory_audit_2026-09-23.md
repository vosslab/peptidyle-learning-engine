Screenshot shorthand: **4 viewports** means laptop, tablet, phone and square captures. **Entry only** means a capture shows the page or a collapsed panel, not the repeated records. **Source only** means no capture visibly confirms that collection.

## Current RecordList boundary

[RecordList](../../../src/components/record_list/record_list.tsx) renders a flat `role=list` of `role=listitem` records. Its shared regions support identity, metadata, status and actions; aligned grid tracks; responsive priorities; optional visible headings; and common loading, empty and error states. The headings are `aria-hidden`, so they do not provide semantic table-column relationships. Responsive regions are hidden at 64rem, 48rem and 32rem breakpoints according to priority.

Presentation variants, reordering and windowing are separate compositions: callers own variant controls and data state; [RecordListReorder](../../../src/components/record_list/record_list_reorder.tsx) supplies move controls and announcements but not persistence; [record_list_window.ts](../../../src/components/record_list/record_list_window.ts) supplies calculations but not measurement or DOM integration. RecordList does not own search, filtering, sorting, paging or selection state.

The proven patterns in the [active migration plan](../active/record_list_migration_plan.md) are E1 simple scan, E2 dense visual table, E3 library search/windowing, E4 reorder, E5 action rows, E6 Student Coursework and E7 gallery/list selection.

## Audit conclusion

RecordList is already a useful shared layer for flat scans, action rows, selectable results and galleries. The refreshed Gradebook capture confirms the new shared region headings improve visual alignment; semantic table relationships remain a separate need. The strongest direct migration set is the unconverted Course, Assessment, Question and Blueprint scan rows; the Student Course and invitation cards; and simple history/activity lists.

The genuine framework gaps are narrower: semantic tables for Gradebook and roster, ordered-list semantics for sequences, a nested-record presentation for Blueprint structure, and an expanded presentation for long review or recovery units. Discussion Posts and rich Question review content still have distinct content and state responsibilities; those should remain caller-owned even if their repeated presentation gains a shared pattern. PageFrame provides the shared outer frame; most remaining differences are in local row/card markup rather than the page shell.

The current screenshots cover visible Student collections across four viewports. Due Soon's capture is empty, and several Blueprint, recovery, discussion and editor candidates are source-only or below the captured viewport. This audit update changed no source files, and no tests were run.
